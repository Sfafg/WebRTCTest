Add the address of each broadcaster you want to watch — every address becomes its own tile, and you can watch several at once.
Pick what to share — everyone who adds your address (shown above) receives it. Any number of viewers can watch at the same time.
Note: browsers only allow screen capture on localhost/HTTPS pages — open this page as http://localhost:8080 on the broadcasting machine.
Resolution: 50%

Bitrate: 1.0 Mbps
Resolution scales the native capture size (100% = full, works for any odd/ultrawide/window size). 60/120 fps needs a display that fast and more CPU/GPU. Bitrate is a cap for each viewer. Changes apply live to everyone watching.
Drag a stream’s top bar to move it, drag its lower-right corner to resize.
